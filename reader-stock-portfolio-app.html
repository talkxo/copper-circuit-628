A PORTFOLIO YOU CAN actually read.

A stock market research app, built to visualize a portfolio the way an investor actually thinks about it — live price movement, AI-generated context on every name, and a way to compare tickers side by side, without switching tabs to piece it together. The product was later acquired and now lives on as a component inside a larger trading platform.

Client
Stock market research app
Category
Product design
Scope
Portfolio & research UI
Focus
Data made legible

The brief was to make a retail investor's portfolio feel like something they could actually read at a glance — not a spreadsheet, not a wall of tickers. Below is that product, section by section, with the thinking behind each screen.

01 · The stock page

The news summary sits next to the chart, not behind a tab.

An AI-generated read of what actually moved the stock today, timestamped, sitting directly beside the price — so the "why" and the "what" never need two separate clicks to connect.

02 · Holdings and research, side by side

Owned and watched, kept honestly separate.

"Holdings" and "Potential" are two different lists with two different jobs — what you actually own, and what you're deciding on — instead of one undifferentiated watchlist pretending they're the same decision. Alongside it, a filing gets condensed into plain sentences with the source cited, not a 22,000-word document to scroll through.

03 · Comparing without a blank canvas

The comparison starts suggested, not empty.

Overlaying a few tickers is easy to promise and hard to make useful — the tooltip does the actual work, converting four overlapping lines into one readable number per stock at the exact point you're looking at, while "comparison suggestions" solve the blank-canvas problem before it starts.
