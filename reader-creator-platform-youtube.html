THE LEADS THAT never searched.

An all-in-one platform for content creators wasn't short on search traffic — it was short on buyers. A two-stage YouTube video ad strategy met the same audience earlier: while they were mid-video, comparing payment tools and course platforms, not yet typing a branded search.

01 — The problem

Plenty of traffic. Not enough buyers.

Search wasn't the weak link — the platform ranked, and paid search brought steady volume. The weak link was what that traffic turned into.

Search captures people who already know what they're looking for — "content creator platform," "course selling software" — a narrow, late-stage pool, usually mid-comparison with two other tabs open, arriving with no context for why this platform specifically fit them.

Most of the actual audience — working creators, coaches, people just starting to sell a course — weren't typing that yet. They were earlier in the problem, and search was never going to catch them there.

02 — The insight

The shopping happens while they're watching, not searching.

Someone watching a "Thinkific vs Teachable" comparison isn't casually browsing — they're actively shopping for course-selling software, just doing it on YouTube instead of Google. Someone watching an "Instamojo tutorial" is wrestling with the exact problem the product solves: getting paid reliably. A search campaign only catches these people later, if they ever type a branded query at all.

That meant the addressable audience wasn't a keyword list — it was a list of videos. Competitor tutorials, competitor reviews, direct platform-comparison content, and the adjacent world of people already selling expertise for a living: personal trainers, business coaches, small business owners starting to teach what they know.

03 — The solution

Two ad stages, placed inside the decision itself.

A two-stage YouTube video ad architecture was built around where the audience actually was — not a search results page, but the videos they were already watching.

Stage 1 · Brand awareness

5-second bumper, straight to the USP

An unskippable 5-second ad, just the product's core value — scheduling and getting paid for courses — placed in front of the target audience and the keyword-themed placements below.

Stage 2 · Traffic (retarget)

15s+ non-skippable, product + proof

Anyone who watched the bumper fully, or engaged with it, was retargeted with a longer ad — how the product actually works, testimonials, and a push straight to sign-up.

Placed in front of the exact moment of comparison

Keyword-themed placements ran the ad before videos on these searches — reaching people already comparing tools, not waiting for them to search the category by name.

And in front of the right kind of person

Layered with demographic (22–40, metro cities) and affinity targeting: personal trainers, business coaches, small business owners, people starting a business, and anyone already interested in online courses or online banking.
