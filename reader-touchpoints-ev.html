A funded electric-vehicle brand had riders leaving showrooms excited — then going quiet for three weeks. Personalised Email + WhatsApp journeys, built around who each buyer actually was, turned that silence into signed deliveries.

01 — The problem

Compounding cancellations.

The showroom experience was strong — riders left excited, questions answered, intent clearly high. The two to three weeks after were a void.

No personalised follow-up. No content that matched the model they'd ridden, or the stage of the decision they were in. Just one generic email sequence, built for no one in particular.

Post-test-drive conversion sat at 34%. Cancellations in the consideration window were quietly compounding — and the sales team had no visibility into why buyers were going cold.

02 — The insight

One empty stage, three different buyers.

A buyer-profiling exercise mapped who was actually walking into showrooms — and what happened to them after they left. It didn't just find an under-served stage. It found an almost entirely empty one, with buyers left to fill the gap themselves: third-party reviews, competitor comparisons, forum threads.

The brand had invested heavily in getting riders to the test drive, then stepped aside at the exact moment the decision was being made.

First-time adopter

Range anxiety, running costs

Needs hard numbers — real-world range, charging cost vs. petrol, total cost of ownership — before trust replaces doubt.

Performance upgrader

Coming from petrol, wants proof

Cares about performance data and community proof: rider reviews, comparison numbers, what people like them actually chose.

Urban commuter

Financing clarity, daily efficiency

Wants the boring-but-decisive details: EMI options, real-world efficiency, day-to-day running cost in their own city.

One sequence couldn't serve all three — and it wasn't.

03 — The solution

Three journeys, one voice.

Three distinct nurture journeys were built across email and WhatsApp, each triggered by the vehicle model tested and the buyer profile behind it. Running seven to twelve days post-test-drive, every touchpoint was mapped to the specific doubts that tend to peak in that window.

Email carried the rational case — owner testimonials, cost-of-ownership breakdowns, range data from riders in similar use cases. WhatsApp carried the emotional one — a short video of the exact model test-ridden, brand content, a direct line to a product advisor for live questions. All of it written in the brand's own voice: confident, direct, genuinely useful.
