A low-investment, high-yield CRM funnel built for a global biotech leader — reaching a narrow, expert audience of biotech educators and researchers that a generic funnel would have talked past.

01 — The problem

Wide portfolio, narrow audience.

A trusted, category-spanning product portfolio — sequencing, amplification, profiling, accessories — and a marketing engine that wasn't built for how narrow this particular audience actually is.

Scientific buyers don't behave like a typical B2B audience. There are relatively few of them, each one represents outsized deal value, and they engage almost exclusively with content that names their exact technique. A broad message covering the whole portfolio at once — RNA-seq and DNA-seq and whole-genome amplification and immune profiling and epigenetics, all in one breath — missed most of the audience most of the time.

The team had visibility. They didn't have relevance — and in a market this specific, relevance is the only thing that converts.

02 — The insight

This audience won't self-segment.

Researchers don't sift through a broad catalogue looking for the part that applies to them — they act when something names their exact technique, and move on when it doesn't. That meant the funnel had to do the segmenting for them, not ask them to do it.

Two behaviours stood out. First, a webinar tied to one specific method — on a fixed date, led by a named specialist — earned attention that a generic "learn more" never would. Second, the moment right after someone raised their hand mattered more than any message after it: reply with a generic "browse our site" and the interest cools; reply with their own category, already sorted out for them, and the conversation keeps going.

So the plan became: replace one broad portfolio message with technique-specific webinars as the entry point, and a category-routed nurture sequence as the very next touch.

03 — The solution

Precision content, not reach.

Biotech educators and researchers don't respond to broad-reach demand gen — they respond to specificity: the exact technique, the exact instrument, the exact use case. The funnel leaned on two content formats built for that audience: focused technical webinars to capture interest at the moment it's highest, and a structured nurture sequence that maps each subscriber to the part of the product portfolio they actually came for.
