BUILT FOR THE SEARCH right after the notice arrives.

A US-based tax and accounting practice serving creators, freelancers, and international filers had a website with a real problem: high bounce, barely any time on site, thin lead flow. 01 — The problem

Traffic wasn't the issue. Staying was.

Visitors landed and left — 80 to 82% of them bounced, spending under a minute on the site before going elsewhere. Leads trickled in at a rate that didn't match the visits.

Generic tax blog posts didn't match how people actually arrive at an accounting site's door. Nobody browses tax content for fun. They arrive because something just happened — an IRS notice landed, a 1099 showed up for money they didn't know was taxable, a filing deadline is a week away — and a 1,500-word article about "tax tips for freelancers" doesn't answer the one question they actually have.

02 — The insight

Work backward from the trigger.

Every visitor who wasn't already a client arrived because something happened first. Mapping those triggers backward — from the moment of panic to the search that followed — meant mapping the audience too: young, tech-savvy earners who don't want a phone call, they want an answer right now, without handing over an email address to get it.

So the plan became: for every real trigger, build a tool that answers the question directly, embed it inside a genuinely useful guide, and let the guide's SEO and the tool's usefulness do the work a gated PDF never could.
