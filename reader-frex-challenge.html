A CALL FOR RESEARCH, staged like a launch.

Education Japan partnered with Ritsumeikan University's FREX graduate program — Frontiers of Earth & Space Exploration — to recruit their next cohort of student researchers. The site treats an academic open call like a mission briefing: an orbiting hero, real challenge tracks, real prizes, and one clear form at the end.

Client
Education Japan × Ritsumeikan University
Category
Website design
Scope
Full campaign microsite
Focus
Ambition, then a form

The FREX Future Innovators Challenge asks one real question — how can Earth & Space science improve life on Earth over the next 20 years — and asks students to answer it with an actual research idea, not just a form fill. Below is that site, section by section, with the thinking behind each one.

01 · The hero
FREX Future Innovators Challenge

The hero asks the actual question, not a tagline.

"How can Earth & Space Science improve life on Earth over the next 20 years?" is the entire pitch — a real, answerable question, with the six challenge tracks named below it before anyone's even scrolled.

02 · Why apply
FREX Future Innovators Challenge

Four reasons, not one paragraph of ambition.

Global Platform, Push Boundaries, Top-Tier Mentorship, Sakura Quest — each one a specific, concrete reason to apply, instead of one long paragraph asking students to take the opportunity on faith.

03 · The tracks
FREX Future Innovators Challenge

Six tracks, each with its own case for itself.

Every track states what it is, what skills it touches, and why it matters — in the same three-part shape, so comparing Lunar Surface Exploration against Comm & Navigation takes one scan, not six different reading efforts.

04 · The prize
FREX Future Innovators Challenge

The prize is a stage, not a certificate.

The Grand Prize isn't a cash number — it's ten minutes presenting live to the Ritsumeikan University team at Sakura Quest. For a competition built to recruit researchers, the reward is designed to look exactly like the outcome it's actually selling.

05 · The form
FREX Future Innovators Challenge

The ask is specific, not "tell us about yourself."

University, education level, year of degree, one research track selected from six — every field maps directly to how an admissions team would actually sort applicants, not a generic lead-capture form repurposed for a competition.
